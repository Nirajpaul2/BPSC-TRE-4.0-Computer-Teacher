💡 8 Golden Rules to Score 60+ in Part III (80 Marks)
1. Tier 1 Priority: Solve Python, DBMS, and Networks questions first to lock in 40+ early points.
2. Manual SQL Tracing: Always draw intermediate tables for `INNER JOIN` and `GROUP BY` on rough paper.
3. OS Gantt Charts: Draw Gantt timeline before computing Turnaround Time (TAT) and Waiting Time (WT).
4. Option (E) Trap: Read all options carefully; BPSC uses "None of the above / More than one of the above" frequently.
5. Python Slicing Boundaries: Verify negative step rules (`start > stop`) for non-empty slices.
6. Protocol Port Memorization: Learn the 10 core ports (80, 443, 20/21, 22, 23, 25, 53, 67/68, 110, 143).
7. Eliminate & Estimate: If you can eliminate 2 out of 4 options, the statistical expectation is positive.
8. Don't Over-study Minor Areas: MS Office & Computer History give only 3-4 marks combined. Don't waste weeks.
