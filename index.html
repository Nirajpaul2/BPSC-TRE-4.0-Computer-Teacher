BPSC TRE 4.0 Computer Science (PGT)

Part I: Language 30 Marks

Qualifying Only (30% = 9 Marks): 8 Questions English + 22 Questions Hindi/Urdu/Bangla. Marks are not counted in merit ranking.

Part II: General Studies 40 Marks

Merit Added: Elementary Mathematics, Mental Ability & Reasoning, General Science, Bihar Special GK & Modern Freedom Movement, Indian Geography.

Part III: Computer Science ⭐ 80 Marks

The Rank Decider: 80 Domain Questions covering 12 structured units. Python (OOP/File I/O), DBMS & SQL, Networks, and Data Structures drive ~60% of questions.

Understanding how qualifying marks progressed across previous Teacher Recruitment Exams (TRE) is vital for setting your preparation targets for TRE 4.0.

Exam Cycle Exam Date General (Unreserved) EWS EBC / BC SC / ST Competition Dynamics
TRE 1.0 (Advt 26/2023) Oct 2023 39 / 120 (32.5%) 39 / 120 39 / 120 39 / 120 More vacancies than STET passouts; almost all qualified were selected.
TRE 2.0 (Advt 27/2023) Dec 2023 63 / 120 (52.5%) 56 / 120 55–60 / 120 40–47 / 120 New STET batches entered. Cutoff leaped +24 marks.
TRE 3.0 (Advt 22/2024) Jul 2024 73+ / 120 (60.8%) 68+ / 120 66–70 / 120 52–58 / 120 Fierce competition; question standard raised to NCERT depth & code tracing.
🎯 TRE 4.0 (Target) Upcoming 85–95+ / 120 (70–80%) 80–88 / 120 78–86 / 120 65–75 / 120 Cumulative STET candidates + repeaters. Mastery of Python & DBMS is mandatory.
💡
Preparation Strategy for 85+ Marks:
• Part III (Computer Science - 80M): Target at least 65+/80 by securing full marks in Tier 1 (Python, DBMS/SQL, Networks).
• Part II (General Studies - 40M): Target 25–30/40 in Elementary Maths, Reasoning, and Modern Bihar History.
• Part I (Language - 30M): Secure 9 marks comfortably to avoid disqualification.
